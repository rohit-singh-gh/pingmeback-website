Delete your PingMeBack account

This removes your account and everything in it, permanently.

If you still have the app

No form needed, happens immediately.

  1. Open PingMeBack
  2. Settings → Delete account
  3. Enter your password and confirm

If you no longer have the app

Use the form with the email address you signed up with. We can't verify an address from a web form alone, so a person checks each request — done within 30 days, usually sooner.

What gets deleted

Your profile, conversations, every message you sent, and every photo, file and voice note you uploaded. Messages you sent are removed from the other person's device the next time it syncs.

Cannot be undone; no backup to restore from. Signing up again with the same email creates a new, empty account — your @username becomes available for anyone to take.

What is kept, and why

Reports you filed about another account are kept as moderation records with your identity removed. Server logs may retain a request trace briefly. Neither contains your messages.

More detail in the Privacy Policy.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. There is no backup to restore from.
