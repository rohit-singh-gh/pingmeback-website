PingMeBack is a one-to-one messaging app. This policy covers what we collect, why, and how to remove it. We don't sell your data, don't share it with advertisers, and there's no advertising or analytics profiling in the app.

What we collect

DataWhyKept until
Email address Account creation, sign-in, password reset, security alerts You delete your account
Username, display name, about, profile photo So people can find and recognise you. Name, photo and last seen each have a visibility setting you control You delete your account
Messages, photos, files, voice notes you send Delivering them You delete them, or your account
Contacts you add in the app Your in-app address book. Not your phone's contacts — never requested, never read You remove them
Device notification token Push notifications Sign-out or device revocation
Session records: device name + two-letter country of sign-in Showing where you're signed in; alerting you to sign-ins you didn't make. Country only — never your full IP You revoke it
Crash reports Fixing crashes. Stack trace, device model, OS version, internal account id. No message content, name or email Up to 90 days
Reports you file about another user Moderation. Includes a copy of the reported message so it survives deletion Retained as a moderation record

What we deliberately don't collect

  • Your phone's contact list — no permission requested
  • Precise location — no location permission; only the two-letter country above
  • Your full IP address — rate limiting uses a salted, irreversible hash
  • Advertising IDs — no ad or third-party analytics SDK
  • Your photo library — attachments go through the system picker, which returns only the file you chose

Security

  • HTTPS in transit
  • The copy on your phone is in an encrypted database, key held in the device's hardware-backed keystore
  • Row-level access rules server-side, so one account can't read another's data
  • Passwords never stored in readable form

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They're encrypted in transit and at rest but readable on the server.

Processors

Supabase (database, file storage, auth) · Google/Firebase (push, crash reporting) · Resend (account and security email). Plus legal disclosure where required.

Your choices

  • Settings → Privacy: who sees your name, photo, last seen; read receipts on/off
  • Settings → Blocked: blocked people can't message you or see your profile
  • Delete messages for yourself or everyone; clear conversations
  • Settings → Delete account: immediate and permanent. If you no longer have the app, use the deletion page

Children

Not directed at anyone under 13. Contact us if a child has created an account.

Changes

Material changes update the date at the top; changes to what we collect are announced in the app.

Email: support@pingmeback.sbs
