FAQ

Questions, answered

Is PingMeBack free?

Yes. The app is completely free during the testing programme, and there are no ads anywhere in it.

Which devices are supported?

PingMeBack is an Android app and runs on Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above. An iOS version isn't available yet.

Why do I need to fill a form to download it?

The app is in closed testing on Google Play, which means only email addresses on the tester list can see and install it. The form is how you get on that list — once you're added, you install it from the Play Store exactly like any other app.

How long does approval take?

We add testers in batches. Most requests are processed within a couple of days; you'll be able to install as soon as your Google account is on the list.

What happens to my messages and media?

They're encrypted in transit over HTTPS, and the copy on your phone sits in an encrypted database keyed to your device's hardware-backed keystore. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted — they're readable on the server. There's no advertising or analytics profiling, and we never sell your data. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete account in the app, enter your password and confirm — it's immediate. If you no longer have the app installed, submit a request on our account deletion page. Either way it's permanent and removes your profile along with everything in it.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

Testers are exactly who we want to hear from. Email support@pingmeback.sbs with what you saw and what you expected, and we'll follow up.
